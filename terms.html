1. Who we are

OnSite 310S Inc. ("OnSite 310S", "we", "I") is a mobile pre-purchase vehicle inspection business based in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada. Inspections are performed by a Red Seal 310S Licensed Automotive Service Technician. "You" means the person who books and pays for the inspection.

2. What the service is

A pre-purchase inspection is a professional, visual and electronic assessment of a used vehicle's condition at a single point in time, carried out where the vehicle is parked. Depending on the vehicle and the access the seller allows, it may include:

  • a review of a vehicle history report (such as Carfax Canada) and paint thickness readings on the exterior panels, compared against that history;
  • a computer scan of the vehicle's electronic control modules;
  • a battery and charging system test;
  • tire tread depth and tire age (DOT date);
  • a visual check of the brakes, suspension, steering, underbody, engine bay, fluid leaks, interior, exterior, lamps and glass where visible;
  • a road test, where the seller permits and it is safe and legal to do so.

You receive a written report with photos, my findings, and my professional opinion and recommendation. The report is based on what was observable, measurable and reported on the day of the inspection.

3. What the service is not

  • Not a warranty, guarantee or insurance. We do not promise that the vehicle will be free of defects, will remain reliable, or will not need repairs after the inspection. Any future failure, wear, breakdown or repair is not covered by OnSite 310S.
  • Not a Safety Standards Certificate (SSC). Only a licensed Motor Vehicle Inspection Station in Ontario can issue one. Where the report lists findings that "could stop the vehicle from passing a safety", that is an opinion, not a pass or fail.
  • Not an emissions test, appraisal or valuation. We do not tell you what the vehicle is worth or what price to pay.
  • Not legal, financial or insurance advice. Information about Ontario dealer disclosure rules is general information only. For a dispute with a dealer, contact OMVIC or a lawyer.
  • Not a repair estimate. We do not quote repair costs.

4. Limits of the inspection

  • Non-invasive. We do not remove panels, trim, wheels, covers or components, and we do not open the engine, transmission, differential or other sealed assemblies. We do not perform compression, leak-down or other internal engine tests unless agreed in writing.
  • Hidden and intermittent problems. Some problems only appear after disassembly, after a certain distance or temperature, under certain weather, or from time to time. These may not be present or detectable during the inspection.
  • Fault codes show what the vehicle's computers report at the time of the scan. Codes that were cleared recently, or problems that have not set a code, will not appear.
  • Conditions on the day (weather, lighting, a cold or warm engine, a dirty vehicle, limited space, or a seller who restricts access or the road test) can limit what can be checked. We note significant limitations in the report.
  • Our equipment is professional-grade and checked and calibrated daily for accurate readings. Measurements are still indications, not exact proof of a cause. For example, a paint thickness reading shows extra paint or filler on a panel, but not always why or how well a repair was done.
  • Third-party information. Vehicle history reports and other third-party data are provided as received. They can be incomplete or wrong, and damage that was never reported will not appear on them. We are not responsible for errors in third-party information.

5. Used vehicles carry risk

Every used vehicle has wear, an unknown history and some risk of future failure. Our job is to reduce that risk with experience, professional knowledge and calibrated tools, and to point out what we find. We cannot remove the risk. Every used vehicle needs regular maintenance and will need repairs over time. You are responsible for budgeting for maintenance, repairs, insurance and the other costs of owning the vehicle.

6. Our recommendation

We only recommend buying a used vehicle that comes with an Ontario Safety Standards Certificate and is covered by an extended warranty from a reputable provider. We do not recommend buying vehicles sold "as-is". A vehicle we mark as "Recommended" or "Recommended if fixed" is recommended on those conditions only. Our recommendation is a professional opinion based on what we found on the day. It does not replace your own judgement.

Inspection terms

By booking an inspection, you agree to these terms.

  1. Purpose. The inspection helps you avoid scam cars, misrepresented history and unreported damage, and shows you the issues to raise before you buy. It is not a promise that nothing will go wrong with the vehicle.
  2. Our recommendation. We recommend buying a used vehicle only with a Safety Standards Certificate and an extended warranty from a reputable provider. We do not recommend vehicles sold as-is.
  3. A snapshot in time. The report describes the vehicle as observed on the inspection date. Its condition can change afterwards.
  4. Non-invasive. We do not remove parts, panels, wheels or interior trim, or open the engine or transmission. Hidden, internal or intermittent problems may not be found.
  5. Not a guarantee. The inspection and report are not a warranty, a Safety Standards Certificate, an appraisal or a guarantee of future reliability or value.
  6. Third-party information. Vehicle history reports and test equipment readings come from third parties. Damage that was never reported will not appear on a history report.
  7. Your decision and costs. The decision to buy, the price you agree to, and all costs of owning the vehicle, including maintenance, repairs and insurance, are your responsibility. Every used vehicle needs maintenance and will need repairs over time.
  8. Access. The inspection depends on the seller allowing access and a test drive. If the seller refuses or the vehicle isn't available at the agreed time, we will work with you to reschedule.
  9. Who can rely on the report. The report is for the client who booked, for the vehicle inspected. Others should not rely on it.
  10. Limit of liability. To the extent permitted by law, OnSite 310S Inc.'s total liability for the inspection and report is limited to the fee paid.
